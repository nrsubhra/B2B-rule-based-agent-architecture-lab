Buying-center agent roles

Four-layer research design

1 · Controlled B2B environment

Researcher-controlled vendor profiles and diagnostic facts.

2 · Lossless design benchmark

Complete information is evaluated directly against the stated organizational objective.

3 · Distributed decision system

The same problem is routed through Single, Pipeline, Parallel, Shared Memory or Supervisor architectures.

4 · Mechanism measurement

Choice, regret, fact retention and intermediate agent reports are recorded.

Why rule-based agents first?

They are controlled mathematical proxies for organizational functions, used to validate the apparatus before LLM-specific randomness, prompting, hallucination or parsing errors are introduced.

They are not claimed to model actual human managers or actual Claude/GPT behavior.

LLM-stage validation

Each source fact should preserve a machine-readable ID. LLMs should return structured fields such as fact_ids_considered and fact_ids_passed_forward. A human-coded validation sample should be used.

The stronger causal test is a fact-removal counterfactual: rerun the identical scenario with one diagnostic fact removed and test whether the decision changes.

Export / share

Scenario JSON contains vendor profiles, critical facts and organizational weights so another person can reproduce or modify your experiment.
