Four-layer research design

1 · Controlled B2B environment

Synthetic vendor profiles and diagnostic facts.

2 · Design-implied benchmark

Complete information is evaluated against the stated organizational objective.

3 · Distributed decision system

The same problem is routed through Pipeline, Parallel, Shared Memory or Supervisor architectures; Single AI is a complete-information reference.

4 · Mechanism measurement

Choice, regret, source availability, transmission, decision availability and intermediate reports.

Why multiple scenarios?

One handcrafted scenario can accidentally favor a particular architecture or vendor. The generator creates many controlled procurement worlds and balances which vendor is benchmark-optimal. Scenario is replication/blocking, not an additional focal manipulation.

Why rule-based agents first?

They are controlled mathematical proxies used to validate whether the apparatus can detect known transmission and integration failures before Claude/GPT-specific prompting, hallucination and stochasticity are introduced.
